# Rātā and the Tree That Returned — original film artwork

Eight original stills generated with ChatGPT's built-in image-generation tool,
not a paid API. The final JPEGs are proportionally cropped to 1080×1920;
no anisotropic stretching is used. `cinematic-system.json` pins their SHA-256.
The images are imaginative illustrations of one pūrākau, not historical
photographs or depictions of an exact customary ritual.

Story basis: Rāwiri Taonui, “Māori relationship with the forest”, Te Ara:
https://teara.govt.nz/en/te-ngahere-forest-lore/page-1 .
The script is original; no source images or prose were copied into the film.

## Image prompt set

Shared direction: one cinematic realistic illustrated portrait per scene;
lush ancient Aotearoa native rainforest; foreground, middle and background
layers suitable for gentle DepthFlow parallax. Keep important action central
for 9:16 framing. No text, border, watermark, modern objects, metal tools or
invented sacred designs. Consistent fictional Rātā: Māori man about thirty,
warm brown skin, dark wavy hair tied back, clean-shaven, no facial tattoos,
short rust-brown flax cloak, practical woven flax garment and waist belt.
Scenes 2–4 and 6–8 used scene 1 as the character/style reference. Scene 5 used
it only as a forest/style reference.

1. Dawn mystery: Rātā stands astonished beside a vast intact tōtara, scattered
   pale woodchips at its roots hinting it was felled the previous day. Stone
   adze at his side, native ferns and amber mist in deep layers.
2. Work begins: kneeling Rātā shapes a freshly felled trunk into a waka with
   a polished stone adze lashed to a wooden handle. Natural hands and tool,
   exposed pale timber, midday shafts of sunlight, rich forest textures.
3. Discovery: closer view of his astonished face and open hand touching the
   intact bark, searching for the vanished cut. Moss, fern foreground and
   pale dawn forest behind; no adze in this shot.
4. Watching: dusk, Rātā crouches behind fern fronds and watches the tree he
   has felled again. The fallen trunk recedes into a blue, moonlit clearing.
5. Restoration: hākuturi represented by native birds and insects gather
   around tree and stump. Tūī, kererū, fantails and beetles amid moss and
   rising woodchips; a restrained golden seam suggests magical restoration.
   No humanoid fairies, neon beams, cartoon eyes or glyphs.
6. Listening: Rātā has emerged and kneels quietly before a tūī on a branch,
   with the restored tōtara behind. His adze rests on the ground away from
   relaxed hands; moonlight, humility, connection, no speech bubbles.
7. Respect: wide dawn portrait, thoughtful Rātā rests an open hand gently
   against tōtara bark, head lowered, tool unused at his waist. Symbolic
   respect only; no claim to show a specific sacred ceremony.
8. Connection: Rātā beside a plain completed dugout waka on calm freshwater,
   one wooden paddle inside, healthy native forest around him. Golden light,
   birds, flax and fern foreground, canoe receding into depth; no motor,
   outrigger or invented ornate carvings.
